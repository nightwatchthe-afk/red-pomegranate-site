Chinese retrieval vectors adapted from giangndm/F2LLM-v2-330M-model2vec
https://huggingface.co/giangndm/F2LLM-v2-330M-model2vec/tree/0b8a351408c77ccd42e1abe409908ae3133d1263
Base model: codefuse-ai/F2LLM-v2-330M. The source model card declares the MIT license.
This site's derivative selects Chinese character and phrase rows, retains 128 dimensions,
and quantizes them to signed bytes for local retrieval.
